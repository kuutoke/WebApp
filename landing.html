What and why?

For well over a decade I've been saving drawings prompts, visual references, userful websites, resources, techniques, and more- all in whatever place and format was most convenient at the time. I remember nothing and haven't been able to find half of my potential saved info; and the ones I have been able to find were saved on Tumblr, which has a notoriously terrible searching and preservation system. Rather than relying on me remembering I have a relevant reference and spending half an hour looking for it any time I sit down to draw, I finally bit the bullet and learned how to make a website. Lacking in a significant amount of references and with buggy code, this hub has at least been established and now has room to grow and develop.

I've been calling it the Artist Reference Hub and often refer to just drawing, but this will hopefully be a place to bounce off from for creation in general. The Drawing tag is for references pulled for and looked at while drawing. Worldbuilding is more about the ideas and questions; it can be a jumping-off point for lore development with something as specified as a name generator or as involved as an index of questions to ask yourself while developing a fictional political structure. The name for "Learning" has changed but I think this encapsulates the idea behind it best: you go here when you aren't actively working on a piece but want to acquire more tools or understanding. It's a catch-all for everything from Clip Studio Paint brushes to websites where you can check the display settings of your monitor.
